# Appendix B: Material Compatibility Matrix for Chamber Components Under Mixed Halogen/Fluorocarbon Exposure

This appendix consolidates the chamber material guidance developed primarily in Chapter 8, organized as a lookup matrix emphasizing OPOP's combined-exposure considerations.

---

## B.1 Plasma-Facing Component Material Compatibility

| Component | Recommended Material | Fluorocarbon Compatibility | Halogen Compatibility | Combined-Exposure Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chamber walls (bulk) | Anodized Al or Y2O3/Al2O3-coated Al | Good (coated) | Generally good (established from logic chamber heritage) | Requires combined-exposure verification per Chapter 8, Part 1 |
| Showerhead | Y2O3-coated Al or Si/SiC | Good | Generally good | Dual-plenum designs may reduce combined-exposure concern, Chapter 6, Section 1.2 |
| ESC surface | Al2O3 or AlN ceramic | Good | Generally good | Edge-exposed regions see both chemistries alternately |
| Focus/edge ring | Quartz, Si, or SiC | Good | Generally good | Lifetime characterization must reflect combined, not additive, exposure (Chapter 8, Section 2.3) |
| Dielectric window (ICP) | Quartz or alumina | Good | Generally good | Standard combined-exposure qualification applies |

## B.2 Byproduct-Specific Thermal Management Requirements

| Byproduct | Boiling Point | Chamber Region Requiring Attention | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| SiF4 | -86°C | None (fully volatile at standard conditions) | None required |
| SiCl4 | 57.6°C | Cooler wall regions in unheated chambers | Modest thermal attention favorable |
| SiBr4 | 153°C | Exhaust path, chamber walls generally | Dedicated heated zones strongly recommended (Chapter 6, Part 3, Part 5) |

## B.3 Gas Delivery Hardware Material Compatibility

| Component | Fluorocarbon-Only Requirement | Halogen-Compatible Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| MFC body/seals | Standard fluorocarbon-compatible elastomers/metals | Halogen-corrosion-resistant materials required (Chapter 6, Section 1.3) |
| Valve seats | Standard | Halogen-corrosion-resistant materials required |
| Gas line material | Standard stainless steel, passivated | Standard stainless steel, passivated; halogen-specific passivation verification recommended |

## B.4 Combined-Exposure Test Protocol Summary (Reference to Chapter 8, Part 5)

| Test Variable | Recommended Levels |
|---|---|
| Exposure sequence order | Fluorocarbon-first, halogen-first, alternating from start |
| Cycle count range | Up to thousands-to-tens-of-thousands of transitions |
| Measurement endpoints | Surface composition (XPS/SIMS), erosion depth, particle generation |

---

*This matrix summarizes guidance developed in Chapter 8; specific material qualification must be performed empirically for each tool platform and OPOP chemistry combination, following the combined-exposure test protocol outlined in Chapter 8, Part 5.*
